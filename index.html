01 / ABOUT ME

Research with people at its centre.

I investigate how technology can make education and everyday learning environments more accessible to people with diverse abilities and needs.

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Computer Science, University of Pisa. My work combines design science research, participatory and human-centred design, prototyping, and empirical evaluation.

From augmented reality for inclusive campus experiences to interactive 3D-printed tactile learning materials, I design systems that make information easier to find, understand, and interact with—while keeping people in control.
